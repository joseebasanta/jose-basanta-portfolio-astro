## The problem

Nimbus's expense-logging flow required seven taps and two screens before a user could save a single expense. Drop-off between "open app" and "expense saved" was over 60%.

## Approach

I ran five moderated usability sessions, mapped the flow against a jobs-to-be-done framework, and prototyped three directions in Figma before converging on a single-screen, camera-first flow.

- Consolidated category, amount, and receipt capture into one screen
- Introduced a reusable component library (buttons, inputs, sheets) adopted by two other product teams
- Partnered with engineering to scope a phased rollout behind a feature flag

## Outcome

- Time-to-log dropped from 40s to 11s
- Weekly active logging increased 34% over the following quarter
- The component library became the seed of Nimbus's current design system
